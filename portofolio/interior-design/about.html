Our Story

Creating Warm Spaces Since 2015

Lumina Interior Studio was founded in 2015 by a group of passionate designers who believed that interior design should be more than aesthetics — it should create feelings, tell stories, and inspire the way people live.

What started as a small studio in Jakarta has grown into one of Indonesia's most respected interior design practices, with projects spanning residential, commercial, and hospitality sectors across the archipelago.

Our name, "Lumina," comes from the Latin word for light. We believe great design illuminates the beauty in everyday living, turning ordinary spaces into extraordinary experiences.

Philosophy

Our Design Philosophy

Three principles guide every project we undertake.

01

Warmth in Every Detail

We believe great design should feel inviting. Every material, color, and texture is chosen to create spaces that welcome you home.

02

Function Meets Beauty

Aesthetic excellence without practicality is merely decoration. We design spaces that work as beautifully as they look.

03

Stories Worth Telling

Every client has a unique narrative. Our role is to translate that story into a physical space that resonates deeply.
