Our Story

About Lumina

Designing warm, artisanal spaces that inspire life — one room at a time.

Est. 2015

From Passion to Purpose

Lumina Interior Studio was founded in 2015 by a group of architects and designers who shared a vision: to create interiors that feel both luxurious and deeply personal.

What began as a small studio in Jakarta has grown into a trusted design house with projects spanning residential penthouses, boutique hotels, and innovative commercial spaces across Indonesia.

Our philosophy is simple — every space should tell a story. We combine natural materials, warm palettes, and artisanal craftsmanship to create environments that elevate everyday living.

Philosophy

Our Design Philosophy

Three pillars that guide every project we undertake

Warmth & Soul

We believe interiors should feel inviting, not sterile. Every material, texture, and color is chosen to create warmth and emotional connection.

Artisanal Craft

We collaborate with local artisans and craftspeople to deliver bespoke pieces that add character and authenticity to every project.

Purposeful Design

Every element serves a purpose. We design spaces that are as functional as they are beautiful — form always follows life.

How We Work

Our Process

A structured approach to bringing your vision to life

1

Discover

2
