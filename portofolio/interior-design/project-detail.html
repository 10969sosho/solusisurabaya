About This Project

The Jakarta Penthouse is a testament to modern luxury rooted in Indonesian warmth. The Wijaya family wanted a space that felt both internationally sophisticated and deeply personal — a home that told their story through every texture, color, and carefully placed object.

We designed open-plan living spaces flooded with natural light, using a palette of warm creams, natural wood, and terracotta accents. The custom teak dining table, handwoven textile art, and locally sourced stone create a dialogue between contemporary design and artisanal craft.

Every room was conceived as an experience — from the meditative master suite overlooking the Jakarta skyline to the intimate family lounge designed for quiet evenings together.
