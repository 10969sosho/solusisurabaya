Our Story

Maison De Lumière was born from a dream — to create a sanctuary where the art of French fine dining meets the warmth of Indonesian hospitality. Founded in 2018 by Chef Laurent Dubois and restaurateur Dewi Kusuma, our restaurant opened its doors in the vibrant heart of Kemang, Jakarta Selatan.

From the very beginning, our mission has been clear: to honor the traditions of classic French cuisine while embracing the incredible diversity of local ingredients. Every dish we serve tells a story — of heritage, of innovation, and of the relentless pursuit of perfection.

Over the years, Maison De Lumière has grown from an intimate 30-seat dining room into one of Jakarta's most celebrated culinary destinations, without ever losing the personal touch that makes every guest feel like family.

The Visionary

Chef Laurent Dubois

Born in Lyon, France — the gastronomic capital of the world — Chef Laurent began his culinary journey at the age of 16 in a traditional boulangerie. After graduating from Le Cordon Bleu Paris, he honed his craft in Michelin-starred kitchens across Europe and Asia.

His philosophy is rooted in three principles: respect for the ingredient, mastery of technique, and the courage to innovate. Before establishing Maison De Lumière, he spent five years as Executive Chef at a two-Michelin-starred restaurant in Tokyo, where he developed his signature style of blending French precision with Asian subtlety.

— Chef Laurent Dubois, Executive Chef & Co-Founder
What Guides Us

Our Philosophy

Ingredient Integrity

We source directly from trusted farmers, fishers, and artisanal producers. Every ingredient is selected at its peak, ensuring that natural flavors shine through in every dish.

Artisanal Craftsmanship

From handmade pasta to house-cured charcuterie, we believe in the beauty of artisanal technique. Our kitchen is a workshop where tradition and creativity coexist.

Hospitality Above All

Fine dining is not just about the food — it's about the entire experience. We treat every guest as an honored friend, ensuring every moment exceeds expectation.
