The Beginning

Born from a Dream

ELVOIRA was founded in 2020 by a young designer with a vision: to bring Indonesian premium fashion to the international stage. Starting from a small studio in Jakarta, ELVOIRA has grown into a globally recognized fashion brand.

Every collection is designed with dedication, blending Indonesian cultural heritage with global fashion trends. We believe that true elegance comes from within, and the right clothing can help every individual express their best self.

Our Craft

Precision in Every Stitch

With a commitment to quality and sustainability, we continuously innovate to deliver collections that are not only beautiful to look at, but also environmentally friendly. Every material is carefully sourced, every stitch placed with intention.

Our design philosophy is rooted in restraint — we believe that true luxury speaks through quality, not excess. Each piece is engineered to transcend seasons and trends.

Our Vision

Shaping the Future

Today, ELVOIRA ships worldwide from Jakarta, bringing premium fashion to discerning customers across the globe. Our three flagship stores in Jakarta, Bandung, and Bali serve as havens for those who appreciate the finer things.

We're just getting started. Our vision is to become Southeast Asia's most respected fashion house — one that proves elegance and sustainability can coexist beautifully.
